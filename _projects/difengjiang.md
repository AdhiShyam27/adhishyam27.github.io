The Difengjiang District Urban Design master plan stretches across 2.97 square kilometres in China's Fujian Province. The triangular site is surrounded by a jasmine field, a Taoist temple, heritage trees and a 4.7-kilometre-long riverfront.

Difengjiang District leverages its natural ecology and rich culture to propose an **active and vibrant waterfront town**. Envisioned as a mixed-development area that encourages physical activities such as walking, the urban design interweaves scenic waterways and greenbelts among the buildings.
