Across 123 square kilometres, Yuzhong Eco Innovative City is a strategic master plan for mixed-use developments in Lanzhou, China. The site is surrounded by Qinglong Mountain in the east, Baihu Mountain in the west, Xinglong Mountain in the south and Bei Mountain in the north.
