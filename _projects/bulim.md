The master plan for Jurong Innovation District integrates various streams of existing-condition analysis, visioning and planning considerations. It can be viewed as an amalgamation of layers that eventually result in a comprehensive land use and gross floor area (GFA) program. These layers include transportation, land use, green network, infrastructure and utilities.

The spatial distribution of parcels and land use involved an iterative process between layers, informed by detailed interactions with the client teams as well as pre-consultation meetings with authorities. Chief among the factors shaping the plan was the need for pragmatic solutions that are implementable and aligned with the client's phasing expectations to cater to industry demand.

Fostering innovation is the cornerstone of the vision for JID. The spatial plan, landscape and transportation infrastructure are aligned to encourage collaboration, exchanges and interactions. Activity nodes and collection spaces help share and disseminate information and data, a smart network harnesses those nodes for interactive display, and multiple modes of transportation let workers and visitors move around easily. The framework is expected to harness the presence of academia, incubators, R&D and industry across sectors and disciplines.
