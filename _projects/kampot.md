Kampot Masterplan is envisioned as a touristic destination and a local community waterfront island. The development creates waterfront resorts, residential communities and a **world-class marina** to handle super yachts berthing or in transit.

Visitors can pick from a range of 3-star to superior 5-star hotels, offering one-of-a-kind positioning overlooking the views of the Gulf of Thailand. The master plan also includes a town centre that will be the heart of the development.
