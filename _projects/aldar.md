Leveraging the site's proximity to Abu Dhabi International Airport, the Aldar Aviation & Logistic Hub master plan envisioned a high-tech logistics hub with a mixture of affordable grade B offices, accommodation and R&D spaces. The project aligned to Abu Dhabi's thematic vision of innovative tactical planning, with a unique industrial co-working zone infused with nature's sustainable revitalization.

The project is designed under three thematic principles: **Technology, Community and Sustainability**. It diverged into an array of components such as mobility, automation, accessibility, clean energy and green innovation. World-leading technology and logistics emphasize multidisciplinary cooperation, access to researchers and students, and international attractiveness, while aiming for an open and inclusive community through accessible open space and innovative meeting and public spaces.

In terms of sustainability and environment, the project highlighted the value of preserving biodiversity, creating health-promoting recreation, promoting emission-free mobility, and introducing renewable energy and zero-emission solutions.
