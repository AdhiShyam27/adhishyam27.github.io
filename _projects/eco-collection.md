The Eco Collection master plan in Phnom Penh is a mixed-use development stretched across 200 hectares. Different villa types, a club house, entertainment zones, retail amenities and themed green spaces are integrated into the space, together with an extensive pedestrian network, public space and a cycling track with distinctive landscaping.

Eco Collection is master planned to redefine liveability. The design is adaptable to the changing demands of homeowners and the ecological landscape of the surrounding area. Committed to sustainability, the multiphase project creates a **complete community** with all basic services, and interweaves water and greenery with buildings.
