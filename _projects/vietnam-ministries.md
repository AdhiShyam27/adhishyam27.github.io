With a vision of designing a landmark for the offices of 12 Vietnam ministries that leaves a legacy for the future, the design emphasizes the **visual connection to West Lake** by creating a linear east-west pedestrian landscape, surrounded by ministry buildings that showcase a formal and timeless architectural language.

The 35-hectare public service institution complex features workspaces that foster innovation and creativity, connected with the local nature and culture.
