As the second-largest island in Batam, Rempang holds the potential to be the new engine of the Batam regional economy, emphasized by its proximity to Singapore and Malaysia. The master plan is designed to foster a robust **renewable energy and tourism-based economy** along the Strait of Malacca.

The plan seeks to balance development with environmental and ecological considerations, conservation and green industry, intending to be both a source of pride and revenue for Batam and to set a high standard for sustainable development. It sets a feasible phased growth and enhances the site's activity offerings, development potential and tourism investment for years to come.

The master plan introduces development while preserving and enhancing the unique ecological and cultural qualities of the area, identifying a realistic development scenario that responds to market constraints, accounts for resource limitation and protects natural resources.
