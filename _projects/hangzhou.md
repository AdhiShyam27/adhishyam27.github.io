Development of the structural plan and urban design for Hangzhou Expo Town, across a 30 square kilometre study area and a 4.2 square kilometre urban design area.

As design lead, the work moved through every stage of design development: **vision development** after detailed analysis of the site and context; an **urban spatial structure and concept master plan** (green and blue structure, infrastructure plan, land use plan and phasing plan); **refinement** after workshops with the client and stakeholders; an **urban design stage** with idea-sharing workshops, urban design guidelines, building design, public space design and landscaping; **massing development**; and review of massing and draft renders to align with the architectural concept.
