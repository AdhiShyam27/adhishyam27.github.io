Changchun North-East Asia Smart Innovative Model District (ASIMD) is a master plan project in China's Jilin Province, stretched across 4.57 square kilometres. Aimed to be the prime smart and innovative district in the northeast of China, ASIMD offers a conducive **test-bedding ecosystem** that enables businesses to thrive in a new digital economy.

To create a vibrant and inclusive district with cutting-edge technology and social innovation, a business park, university, medical centre and community facilities were integrated to encourage greater collaboration between industry and academia. Considering Changchun's climate, the plan provides seamless under- and above-ground connections between buildings and transport nodes. These multi-level connections, which also serve as public space, are designed around a lake as the focal point, creating a vibrant waterfront development.
