Designed to be a culmination of entertainment, RSP proposed the Festival LIVE development. Created in the south end of Dubai Festival City, the project aims to harness the existing and future circulation flows of its surrounding developments to create a new spectacular city hub.

The concept aims to attract people of all ages, locals and tourists alike, to come and enjoy its unique street atmosphere, new to Dubai as another lifestyle entertainment destination. The master plan includes a multitude of experiential nodes and events that visitors can traverse.

With **harmony and integration** as the key aspects of the concept, the various spatial experiences are designed to interflow with one another, blending the worlds of retail, F&B and leisure and representing the future of retail developments.
