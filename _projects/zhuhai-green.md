The Zhuhai Xiangzhou District Green Connector System master plan spans 80 square kilometres in China's Guangdong Province. Accessibility, connectivity and green elements were the main areas of focus.

A **multi-layer connectivity system** was designed for ease of travel. At the city level, transportation systems were implemented. Within the district, cycling tracks and walking trails were designed with the green aspect in mind, encouraging a healthier lifestyle and a vibrant, happy city.
