Urban design guidelines (UDG) translate a master plan into rules that shape the physical built form and environment. For the Jurong Innovation District they were organized as **primary guidelines** (mandatory requirements) and **secondary guidelines** (encouraged requirements), across urban and architectural design.

The guideline set moves from the district scale down to individual parcels: structure plan and base plan; land use plan and access and road network; open space network and action zones; retail distribution; setbacks and pedestrian network; building envelope and roofscape; and parcel-specific guidelines covering vehicular access and drop-off points, platform levels and mitigation zones, façade and roofscape control, building envelope and view corridors, public accessible plazas and active zones, and covered walkways at deck level.
